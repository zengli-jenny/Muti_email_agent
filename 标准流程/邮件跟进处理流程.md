# 总纲

当会话被标记为“待跟进”状态后，由客服专家智能体自动**检查上一封客服的回复内容并按照以下类型生成对应的跟进邮件，跟进邮件的核心目标是确认问题是否已解决或流程是否已闭环，而非重新响应客户诉求或开启新处理流程。**  不主动提供新方案、不重新解释问题，仅表达关心与确认。邮件内容严格参考【邮件生成流程】。

---

# 售后处理类跟进

#### 已执行换货、补发

* 跟进重点：确认客户是否已收到货。

* 回复示例：This is [客服名称] to follow up on your case. I notice that the replacement has been delivered to your address. Have you received it? How does it work for you? Should you have any issue, please don't hesitate to let us know. Thanks.

> 若查询不到物流信息，则参考回复示例直接询问客户是否收到货即可，无需请求人工客服支持。

---

#### 已执行退款

- 跟进重点：确认客户是否已收到退款。

- 回复示例：This is [客服名称] to follow up on your case. Have you received the refund? Do you need more help? In the future, we will keep improving this product for a better customer experience, which is the goal and driving force here in 品牌. Should you have any issue, please don’t hesitate to let us know. Thanks.

> 

---

#### 提供过技术支持后客户未回复

- 跟进重点：跟进咨询技术支持是否解决问题（如有订单号等其他信息问询可根据情况拓展邮件内容）。

- 回复示例：This is [客服名称] to follow up on your issue. We once replied to your email with some technical support. Did you receive and whether you troubleshoot as instructed? How does the xx work for you now? If there is still the issue after you do all, please just let us know, we will further resolve it for you/we will send you a replacement. Thanks.  （需要客服更新）

---

#### 客户问题描述不清或者不够详尽无法判断客户问题，询问后客户未回复。

- 跟进重点：跟进询问问题细节。

- 回复示例：This is [客服名称] to follow up on your issue. We once replied to your email and asked if you could provide a detailed description of the issue. But we are sorry we still haven’t heard back from you yet. Could you please let us know more about the problem and any troubleshooting steps you may have already taken to see if any technical support would help? Thanks.

---

#### 客服请客户提供更多信息，客户未回复。

- 跟进重点：根据客服发送的上一封邮件跟进。

---

#### 问题明确可换货，但未提供订单号和地址。

- 跟进重点：跟进要订单号和地址。

- 回复示例：This is [客服名称] to follow up on your issue. We once replied to your email and asked for your order number and shipping address, but we still haven’t heard back from you yet. Could you please get back with those information so we can go further? Thanks.

---

#### 承诺给客户提供退货标签但客服还未提供。

- 跟进重点：跟进提供退货标签。

- 回复示例：Attached please find the prepaid shipping label and arrange the return at your convenient time. Thanks for your cooperation.

---

#### 客户订单过保，客服拒绝承保后客户不回复。

* 回复示例：We once replied to your email but we still haven’t heard back from you yet. Even though we cannot provide a replacement or refund for the product due to the warranty expiration, we would like to offer you a special discount on your next purchase as a gesture of goodwill. Please inform us of the item you would like to order before placing the order, and we will determine the best discount we can offer. Thank you.

---

#### 客户订单过保，客服提供复购折扣方案后客户不回复。

* 回复示例：We once replied to your email but we still haven’t heard back from you yet.Since we regard you as our valued customer, even though your order has expired the warranty, we still would like to send you a new replacement or issue you a refund as a one-time courtesy.

---

# 投诉类跟进

* 跟进重点：先致歉或表示理解  +  简要回顾之前的处理或说明 + 询问是否还存在未解决的问题。 

---

# 物流类跟进

#### 物流查询/延误/未收到/异常

- 跟进重点：提供最新的物流跟踪信息 + 询问客户是否已收到包裹。

---

#### 已告知客户我们将联系物流公司调查

从告知我们联系物流公司调查回复邮件发送时间至今计算周期：

- 若周期 >= 14天：告知客户给他重新发货，**询问客户意见**，查看订单基本信息中"收件详细地址（address）"，如果为空则请客户提供完整收货信息（包括`name`、`address`、`city`、`state`、`country`、`zip`、`phone number`，尤其是在亚马逊上购买我们一般无法获取到地址），如果有值则请客户确认地址，等待客户回复。
  
  ---

- 若周期 < 14天：询问人工客服调查结果。
