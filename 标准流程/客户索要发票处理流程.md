# Step 1. 查询订单状态

【必须执行】调用`查询订单基本信息`工具查询订单状态。**无论之前邮件查询的订单状态是什么，必须重新查询订单状态**，避免因信息过期导致错误回复。

---

# Step 2. 判断订单退款状态

* 若订单退换货状态 replace_status = `Refunded` 或 replace_status = `Replaced+Refunded` → 告知客户该订单无法提供发票。回复示例：Unfortunately, we are unable to provide an invoice for this order as it has been fully refunded.
  
  ---

* 否则 → Step 3.

---

# Step 3. 判断订单状态

- Status = `canceled`：告知客户该订单无法提供发票。回复示例：We regret to inform you that we are unable to provide an invoice for this order, as it has been canceled.

---

- Status = `pending / prepared / printed / hold / outofstock / scan / partprinted`：
  
  - **【特殊情况】若购买平台为亚马逊 且 渠道对应国家为`美国(US)、加拿大(CA)`：建议客户去亚马逊后台下载发票，执行【亚马逊平台美国、加拿大下载指引】**。
    
    ---
  
  - 其他国家：告知客户订单尚未完成交付，暂无法提供发票，订单完成后可提供。回复示例：1. Please note that the invoice cannot be issued at this time as the order has not been fully delivered. We will provide the invoice once the order is completed.  2. Thanks for ordering from us. As your order is still in payment pending status, the invoice is unavailable at the moment. We'll send you the invoice after your order is fulfilled. Thanks.

---

- Status = `closed`  ：
  
  - 若购买平台为亚马逊（platform=`Amazon`） → 执行【Amazon平台处理流程】。
    
    ---
  
  - 若购买平台为官网（platform=`官网`） → 执行【官网平台处理流程】。

---

# Amazon平台处理流程

1. 若渠道对应国家为`欧盟(EU)、英国(UK/GB)、印度(IN)`：
   
   * 我们直接给客户提供发票，告知客户见邮件附件。回复示例：Thanks for ordering from us. Attached please find the invoice of your order 订单号.
     
     ---
   
   * 若客户有修改需求：告知客户发票由Amazon直接提供，需要客户直接联系Amazon修改。回复示例：We participate in the Amazon Tax Calculation Service, so the invoice is provided by amazon on behalf of us seller. If you would like to change any information on the invoice, please contact amazon directly on this request. Thank you.
     
     ---  
   
   * 若客户坚持必须修改（**仅在客户反复强调需要修改的情况适用**）：人工客服处理。

---

2. 若渠道对应国家为`巴西(BR)、新加坡(SG)`：
   
   * 若客户未提供姓名、地址等发票信息： 请客户提供姓名、地址。回复示例：Could you please provide your full name and billing address for the invoice?
     
     ---
   
   * 若已提供姓名、地址：我们直接给客户提供发票，告知客户见邮件附件。回复示例：Thanks for ordering from us. Attached please find the invoice of your order 订单号. Thank you.

---

3. 若渠道对应国家为`阿联酋(AE)、墨西哥(MX)、澳大利亚(AU)`：
   
   * 我们直接给客户提供发票，告知客户见邮件附件。回复示例：Thanks for ordering from us. Attached please find the invoice of your order 订单号. Thank you.
     
     ---
   
   * 若客户有修改需求且已提供修改信息：我们直接给客户提供发票，告知客户见邮件附件。回复示例：Thanks for ordering from us. Attached please find the invoice of your order 订单号. Thank you.
     
     ---
   
   * 若客户有修改需求但未提供修改信息：请客户提供修改信息，收到后提供发票。回复示例：Could you please provide your full name and billing address for the invoice?

---

4. 若渠道对应国家为`美国(US)、加拿大(CA)`：建议客户去亚马逊后台下载发票，执行【亚马逊平台美国、加拿大下载指引】。

---

# 亚马逊平台美国、加拿大下载指引

-  美国下载指引：You can print an invoice for your order in Your Account as soon as your order is shipped.
  
  1. Go to Your Orders and select Invoice underneath the order number. 
  2. Select Print this page for your records on the top of the order summary. 
  3. Select your browser's print button.
  
  ---

- 加拿大下载指引：
  
  You can print an invoice for your order in Your Account as soon as your order is shipped.  
  1. Go to Your Orders and select Invoice underneath the order number. 
  2. Select Printable Order Summary in the drop-down menu. 
  3. Select your browser's print button.

---

  ---

* 若已提供姓名、地址：我们直接给客户提供发票，告知客户见邮件附件。回复示例：Thanks for ordering from us. Attached please find the invoice of your order 订单号. Thank you.
  
  ---

  ---

* 若客户有修改需求但未提供修改信息：请客户提供修改信息，收到后提供发票。回复示例：Could you please provide your full name and billing address for the invoice?

---

# 限制

* 所有流程禁止主动向客户询问是否需求修改发票信息。
